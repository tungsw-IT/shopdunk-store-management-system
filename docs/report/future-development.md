# Future Development

This document summarizes the future development directions proposed for the ShopDunk Store Management System.

The future roadmap focuses on mobile applications, payment integration, artificial intelligence, and omnichannel sales management.

---

## 1. Mobile Application Ecosystem

The system can be expanded with mobile applications for both customers and internal employees.

### 1.1 Customer Mobile Application

A customer-facing mobile application can provide functions such as:

- Manage personal profile
- Manage customer account
- Track loyalty points
- Track repair and warranty progress
- Receive personalized promotions
- Access customer services directly from a mobile device

### Expected Business Value

The mobile application can help:

- Improve customer convenience
- Increase customer engagement
- Improve access to after-sales services
- Support personalized marketing
- Strengthen customer loyalty

---

### 1.2 Employee Mobile Application

An internal application can support employees during daily store operations.

Potential functions include:

- Barcode scanning using the smartphone camera
- Instant inventory checking
- Product lookup
- Inventory information access
- Repair status updates
- Mobile access to operational information

### Example Use Cases

#### Sales Staff

Sales staff can use the smartphone camera to scan a product barcode and immediately check available inventory.

#### Technician

Technicians can update repair progress and repair status from a mobile device.

### Expected Business Value

This can help:

- Reduce manual data entry
- Speed up inventory checking
- Improve employee mobility
- Keep repair information updated
- Improve operational efficiency

---

## 2. Online Payment and E-wallet Integration

The payment module can be expanded to provide more comprehensive electronic payment support.

### Proposed Features

- Dynamic QR Code generation
- Automatic payment confirmation
- Electronic payment integration
- Credit card payment
- Installment payment

### Proposed Payment Services

The future system may integrate with:

- Apple Pay
- VNPay
- MoMo
- Banking services
- Credit card installment systems

---

## 3. Dynamic QR Payment

The Point-of-Sale system can generate a Dynamic QR Code directly on the payment interface.

```text
Invoice Created
