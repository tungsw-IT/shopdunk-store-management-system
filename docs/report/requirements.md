# System Requirements

## 1. Requirement Analysis

The ShopDunk Store Management System is designed to support and manage the main business processes of a mobile phone store.

The requirements were identified through requirement gathering, interviews, surveys, and analysis of the store's business processes.

## 2. Functional Requirements

The system provides the following main functions:

- User authentication and account management
- Customer management
- Employee management
- Product management
- Product category management
- Supplier management
- Inventory and import management
- Shopping cart management
- Order management
- Invoice creation and sales management
- Payment processing
- Warranty and repair management
- Sales monitoring and reporting
- Product search

## 3. Non-functional Requirements

The system considers the following non-functional requirements:

- Security and access control
- System performance
- Data integrity
- Usability
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Compatibility

## 4. Requirement Elicitation

Requirements were collected and analyzed through:

- Stakeholder interviews
- Customer surveys
- Observation of business processes
- Requirement clarification
- Analysis of functional and non-functional requirements

## 5. System Modeling

The analyzed requirements were used as the basis for designing:

- Use Case Diagrams
- Activity Diagrams
- Sequence Diagrams
- Class Diagram
- State Diagrams
- Database Design
- Test Cases and Test Scenarios
