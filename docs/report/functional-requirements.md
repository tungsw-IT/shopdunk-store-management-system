# Functional Requirements

This document summarizes the functional requirements of the ShopDunk Store Management System.

The requirements are organized using requirement IDs from **FR-01 to FR-56** based on the project report.

| FR-03 | Forgot Password |

## Requirement Coverage

The functional requirements cover the main business areas of the system:

- Authentication and account management
- Point-of-Sale operations
- Customer Relationship Management
- Repair and warranty processing
- Employee management
- System administration
- Online customer purchasing
- Product and inventory management
- Business reporting and statistics
