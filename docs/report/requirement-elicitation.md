# Requirement Elicitation

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of requirement elicitation is to understand the actual business processes and identify the requirements for the ShopDunk Store Management System.

## 2. Elicitation Methods

The project used the following methods to collect requirements:

- Interviews
- Customer surveys

## 3. Interview

Interviews were conducted to collect information about the store's business processes and system requirements.

The collected information was analyzed and used to identify system functions and clarify business requirements.

## 4. Customer Survey

Customer surveys were used to understand customer needs and expectations when interacting with the store and its system.

Survey results supported the analysis and clarification of system requirements.

## 5. Requirement Clarification

After collecting information, the requirements were analyzed and clarified before being used for system design.

The results became the basis for:

- Functional Requirements
- Non-functional Requirements
- Use Cases
- System Modeling
- Database Design
- Testing
